Services / Capabilities

Build better.
Ship faster.

I combine hands-on development with product delivery, UX collaboration and performance optimisation. The goal is simple: make the right thing, make it well, and get it live.

01

Websites &
e-commerce.

  • Custom CMS themes, templates and custom builds
  • WooCommerce product, cart and checkout experiences
  • Shopify storefronts and Liquid customisation
  • Builder-to-custom-code refinement
  • Responsive, accessible and conversion-focused UI

I build marketing websites and online stores around business goals rather than templates. From information architecture and content hierarchy to checkout UX, every layer is designed to be maintainable and commercially useful.

02

Product delivery
from idea to launch.

  • Discovery and requirements gathering
  • Technical scoping and implementation planning
  • Coordination with design and development teams
  • Multi-project prioritisation and delivery
  • Launch QA and post-launch optimisation

My lead experience means I can own more than the code. I translate business requirements into clear product specifications, coordinate moving parts and keep delivery focused on outcomes, quality and deadlines.

03

Custom frontend
& backend.

  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP
  • Custom APIs and third-party integrations
  • MySQL and server-side logic
  • Custom CMS hooks, filters and integrations
  • CMS architecture and technical troubleshooting

When a plugin or theme cannot deliver the right experience, I work at the code level. That includes frontend interactions, PHP logic, API connections, database-backed workflows and CMS architecture.

04

SEO, speed
& Core Web Vitals.

  • Technical SEO audits and implementation
  • Schema markup and sitemap optimisation
  • Core Web Vitals and PageSpeed improvements
  • Caching, CDN and asset optimisation
  • Image, CSS and JavaScript performance

Performance is part of development, not a final checklist. I work on the underlying causes of slow pages: caching strategy, assets, code, server behaviour, layout stability and technical SEO foundations.

05

Figma-to-code
UI engineering.

  • Pixel-accurate responsive implementation
  • Design-system-aware component styling
  • Micro-interactions and motion
  • Mobile-first responsive behaviour
  • Design and development handoff and iteration

I turn Figma and visual direction into production-ready interfaces without losing the details that make the design feel premium. The focus is consistency, responsiveness and interactions that support the content rather than distract from it.
